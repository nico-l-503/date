Coffee, a Walk, and Cow Tools

From Crushipedia, the free encyclopedia. (Redirected from Please Say Yes)
This article is about a proposed outing involving caffeine, ambulation, and an extended tangent about a 1982 Far Side comic. For the physiological sensation, see Butterflies (idiom). For the comic itself, see Cow Tools (Far Side).

Coffee, a Walk, and Cow Tools is a proposal, first drafted moments ago, under consideration by exactly one recipient[1]. The itinerary is deliberately low-stakes[citation needed]: one (1) coffee, one (1) walk, and an unspecified but likely excessive amount of time spent discussing "Cow Tools," the infamous 1982 Far Side comic that nobody, including its own creator, has ever fully explained[2].

Unlike most encyclopedia entries, this one has a deadline. Scholars[citation needed] note that the proposal was drafted using a genuinely quirky, early-internet aesthetic, on the theory that sincerity lands better when it's dressed up as a bit, and that no first date has ever been improved by less cow-related confusion.

The subject of the proposal is referred to throughout this article as NAME, per standard Crushipedia naming conventions.
