Will You Go Out With Me?

From Crushipedia, the free encyclopedia. (Redirected from Please Say Yes)
This article is about a proposed evening plan. For the physiological sensation, see Butterflies (idiom). For the 2004 board game, see Speed Dating (game).

Will You Go Out With Me? is a proposal, first drafted moments ago, under consideration by exactly one recipient[1]. It is widely regarded as a low-risk, high-upside venture[citation needed], typically involving food, conversation, and an above-average amount of nervous laughter[2].

Unlike most encyclopedia entries, this one has a deadline. Scholars[citation needed] note that the proposal was drafted using a genuinely quirky, early-internet aesthetic, on the theory that sincerity lands better when it's dressed up as a bit.

The subject of the proposal is referred to throughout this article as NAME, per standard Crushipedia naming conventions.
